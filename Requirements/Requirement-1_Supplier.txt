Purpose: Store supplier details and their contractual SLA (Service Level Agreement) conditions.

The class should contain the following fields:
1. supplierId – Unique ID of the supplier.
2. supplierName – Name of the supplier.
3. monthlyValue – Total monthly contract value.
4. otifTarget – Minimum required OTIF percentage (e.g., 95%).
5. gracePeriodDays – Additional grace period allowed (e.g., 2 days).
6. penaltyRate – Penalty percentage applicable to eligible late orders.
7. penaltyCapPercentage – Maximum monthly penalty percentage (e.g., 5%).
2. Required methods
- Constructor to initialize all supplier details.
- Getters to access supplier information.
- Setters to update supplier information if required.
- toString() to display supplier details.
3. Service class requirements
DeliveryEvaluationService.java

No service logic is required inside Supplier.java.

Later, the service class will use the supplier's SLA details to:
- Retrieve the grace period.
- Retrieve the OTIF target.
- Retrieve the penalty rate.
- Retrieve the monthly penalty cap.
- Evaluate orders according to the supplier's agreement.
